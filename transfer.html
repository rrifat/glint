Take your highlights with you

Read on your iPhone, or move your library to another browser.

Export highlights

Exports include saved quotes, notes and source links. Save any unfinished note edits first.

On your Mac, open Notes → File → Import to Notes and select the text file. Keep the imported note in your iCloud Notes folder, with Notes enabled in iCloud on both devices, to read it on your iPhone.

Text and Markdown exports are reading copies. Importing them again creates another copy. They do not update automatically or restore highlights on websites. Only a Glint JSON backup can be imported back into Glint.

Import a Glint backup

Move highlights from Firefox to Chromium, or restore a backup. Existing highlight IDs are skipped, keeping your local colours and notes. This adds missing highlights; it does not sync edits or deletions. An older backup can bring back deleted highlights.

After import, open the original page with Glint enabled. It will attempt to restore each saved passage when the matching text is loaded.
